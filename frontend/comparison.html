Differences between the two models

AspectConditional GANConditional VAE
IdeaGenerator and discriminator compete (adversarial game).Encoder compresses to a latent distribution; decoder reconstructs.
Training signalDiscriminator’s real/fake judgement.Reconstruction loss + KL divergence (ELBO).
Typical image qualitySharper details.Smoother, blurrier (pixel-wise averaging).
Training stabilityCan oscillate or mode-collapse.Stable and easy to monitor.
DiversityCan miss some modes.Covers the data distribution more evenly.
Conditioning hereLabel embedding joined to noise; label also given to discriminator.One-hot label joined to the encoder input and decoder input.

These rows describe the general behaviour of the two model families. The images and CLIP numbers above come from this project’s own trained models, so judge them yourself.
